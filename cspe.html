Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is school compulsory in France?

Education is compulsory from age 3 to 16. Families can meet this obligation through schooling or, under applicable rules, other approved arrangements.

What is the difference between collège and lycée?

Collège is lower secondary education, generally for ages 11–15. Lycée is upper secondary, usually for ages 15–18, and leads toward the baccalauréat or another qualification.

What is the baccalauréat?

The baccalauréat, often called “le bac,” is a major qualification at the end of lycée. It can provide access to higher education, subject to program-specific requirements.

Does everyone go to university after lycée?

No. University is one option among several. Students also choose selective institutions, technical programs, apprenticeships, and other forms of training.
